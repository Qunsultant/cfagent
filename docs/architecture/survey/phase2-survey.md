## 7. Biggest duplication findings (for #3123 / #3124 design)

1. **One ledger, two stores, two read APIs, client-side merge.** `job_run_records` is written to OrchestratorAgent DO SQLite (P-A, W-1/W-2/W-5/W-6) *and* to ScheduledAgent DO KV under the `"jobRunRecords"` key (P-B, W-3/W-4), with different caps (100/job vs 500 total), different read shapes (`listByJob`/`listRecent` vs `listJobRuns` over a KV array), and different endpoints (`/api/schedules/runs` vs `/api/schedule/runs`). Both `ScheduleRunLedgerPanel` and `JobsPanel` fetch both and merge in the browser. This is the central #3123 unification: one schedule store implies one ledger store, or at minimum one server-side merged read.
2. **Pause/resume is copy-and-recreate with identity churn, across two stores.** Pausing a T2 schedule cancels the SDK row and copies its definition into `paused_schedules` (S-2); resuming re-arms it as a *new* SDK schedule with a new id (`routes/schedules.ts:509-532`, audit carries `newScheduleId`); updating a live schedule is cancel + re-create the same way (`:581`). The list endpoint then merges the two stores at read time (`:296-306`). Goal pause is a boolean flag in KV (`setGoalEnabled`) — a third, incompatible pause semantic for the same user-visible action.
3. **Two missed-fire watchdogs, two scans, two claim mechanics.** `watchdogMissedSchedules` (E-6/W-5, SDK schedules, SQLite `latestByJob`) and `runScheduleWatchdog` (E-7/W-4, goals, KV `getJobRuns`) detect the same failure mode with separate grace handling, separate heartbeat rows, and separate audit events — and both depend on a tick that is currently not firing (crons disabled).
4. **Five overlapping scans over two tables, each with its own bounds.** `mas_async_jobs` is scanned by recovery-queued (LIMIT 100), recovery-stale-running, and the 15-min stale sweep; `cf_agents_jobs` (via `listSchedules`) is scanned by baseline reconciliation, legacy migration, strategy-scan ensure, duplicate check, and two sweep-arming existence checks — all at `onStart` or per-create, each re-listing the full schedule set. #3124's single-scan target must absorb E-2–E-13, not just the two stale sweeps.
5. **Three management dialects for one concept.** T2 and T3 have separate API families (`/api/schedules/*` vs `/api/schedule/*`), separate client modules (`jobsApi.ts` vs `scheduleApi.ts`), and the T2 clients themselves disagree (direct `fetch` in `SchedulesPanel`/`ScheduledOptionsManager` vs `jobsApi` wrappers); triggers exist in four shapes (generic `/:id/trigger`, seven `trigger-*` system routes, goal `triggerGoal`, A2A/mesh-door triggers). Meanwhile T1 has *no* management surface at all — and is currently disabled entirely (#4112), so the S5 entry points, the record route, and the #2037 tick trace are dead code in production until the crons are uncommented. #3123 must rule on whether the unified store restores the crons first, or designs a scheduler that does not need them.
6. **Run history is three tables, not one ledger.** `job_run_records` (R-1/R-2), `mas_async_jobs` (R-3/R-5), and `mas_job_outcomes` (R-4, Autopilot) each serve a different surface, and `mas_opportunity_scans` remains unjoinable (open #3314). Any "single ledger read path" that only merges R-1+R-2 leaves WorkflowRunsPanel and Autopilot on separate histories.
