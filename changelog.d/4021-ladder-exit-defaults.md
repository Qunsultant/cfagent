### Fixed

- **A bracket ladder can no longer invent its exits (#4021):** a live AAPL ladder was placed with +$1.00 / −$2.00 exits (348.44 / 345.44 on a 347.44 entry, i.e. +0.29% / −0.56%) where +4% / −2% were requested. The cause was a silent default — `take_profit_delta ?? 1.0` and `stop_loss_delta ?? 2.0` — on a money-critical parameter, hit by a caller speaking the #3812 percent vocabulary the tool did not accept. Exits now resolve through `resolveLadderExitDeltas`: absolute deltas, or `take_profit_pct` / `stop_loss_pct` converted against the tier entry to cents; a request that names neither is refused before any rung is placed ("No default exit is invented"), and non-positive distances are refused. Pinned by `tests/bracket_ladder_exit_defaults_4021.test.ts` (5 tests).
