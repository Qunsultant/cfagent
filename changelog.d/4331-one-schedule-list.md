### Changed

- **One schedule vocabulary and one classifier (#4329 S2, #4331):** the schedules list's filter bar is now the owner's ten filters — All, Stocks, Options, Strategy, Goal, Payment, Trading, System, Queued, Other — configured in `src/config/scheduleListModes.ts` (`SCHEDULE_LIST_FILTERS`, `SCHEDULE_LIST_DEFAULT_FILTER`) with the ledger's resolution rules, and every count and filter decision comes from one classifier (`src/services/scheduleKinds.ts`) over the recorded callback, type and payload: a delayed job is Queued whatever it would do, options and strategy precede trading, a universe payload marks Stocks, the platform's own upkeep is System, and an unmatched callback is Other — never dropped, never guessed. A row now names its job by the catalog label (`Stock Market Screen`, `Strategy Discovery · No-view`) instead of the raw callback, with the callback still the fallback for anything unlabelled. #autoclaw-001
