### Added

- **IV history has provenance (#3326, programme #3292):** `IvHistoryRecord` now records which broker's chain produced each observation, and reads are scoped by it, so a tenant that selected Alpaca sees Alpaca's own series — never E*TRADE's relabelled, and never a curve derived from a single snapshot. `normalizeIvBroker` treats a legacy row (and any unrecognised value) as `etrade`, which is what actually produced it. One observation per `(symbol, date)` is preserved: a second broker collecting the same day reports `already_recorded` and does not rewrite the day's provenance. Storage: migration 0045 adds `agent_iv_history.broker` (default `etrade`); the DO adapter probes the column additively, the D1 and in-memory adapters filter by it, and the D1 conformance pin now compares the schema against migration 0020 **plus** the later ALTER rather than editing an applied migration.
- **Every broker collects its own chains (#3326):** the daily IV collection resolves the selected broker. E*TRADE keeps its existing path unchanged; any other broker is read through its own platform options data plane (`platformChainSource`), and a platform without one fails the run with a named error instead of writing nothing silently. `brokerSupports("alpaca", "ivHistory")` is now true, and the IV panel labels the series with its broker and states an empty series as empty ("Nothing is backfilled or relabelled").
