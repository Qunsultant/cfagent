### Changed

- Run Details messages are folded into the single outcome catalog (#2608): the 18 `RUN_*` messages now live in `src/outcomes/outcomeCatalog.json` under a new top-level `runDetailMessages` section and resolve through `src/outcomes/outcomeAspects.ts` (`resolveRunDetailMessage`, `classifyRunDetailError`), so there is no second catalog. `src/services/runDetails.ts` keeps `RUN_DETAIL_MESSAGE_CATALOG`, `resolveRunDetailMessage`, and the message types only as deprecated thin wrappers naming the successor; `buildRunDetail` resolves every reason through the single resolver and now reports `catalogVersion` `"2"` (v1 was the standalone catalog). No wording changes: all 18 entries moved verbatim. Near-equivalent `FAILURE_REPORT_*` outcome codes stay distinct — they are job-outcome codes, while `RUN_*` entries are the run-detail renderings with `{param}` evidence slots and remediation actions.
