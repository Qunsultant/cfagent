### Changed

- Built-in scheduled goals are externalized to `config/builtin-scheduled-goals.json`, read through `src/config/builtinScheduledGoals.ts` (Qunsultant/cfagent#4253): the nine goals no longer live as literals in `src/schedule.ts`, the seed rule seeds from config, removing a built-in goal is durable (recorded under `removedBuiltinGoals`, so re-seed and Seed-all never resurrect it), and `GET /api/schedule/builtin-goals` serves the config minus owner-removed goals. Daily-defaults directive applied in the config: opportunity-hunt is now daily at 07:00 (was every 6h and, under the no-timeZone `isDue` branch, never fired) and resource-cap-alert is now daily at 00:00 UTC (was hourly); feed-refresh is already gone from main (#4350) so it has no daily conversion. No behavior change for the other seven goals.
