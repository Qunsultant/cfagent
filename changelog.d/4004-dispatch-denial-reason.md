### Fixed

- **A dispatch denial now says what to do about it (#4004, incident #4003):** `schedule.dispatch_denied` recorded the reason code and (sometimes) a detail, but the schedules audit trail rendered neither — an operator saw four denials for the stock screen with no way to tell an unowned schedule from a revoked membership. The audit payload now carries an `action` per reason (`NO_IDENTITY`, `SHARED_ADDRESS`, `SERVICE_NOT_CONFIGURED`, `SERVICE_NOT_PROVISIONED`, `SERVICE_TENANT_MISMATCH`, `BROKER_REQUIRED`, `OWNER_MEMBERSHIP_REVOKED`, `PDP_DENIED`), and the audit trail renders a Reason column (reason code, with detail and action on hover). The denial pin in `tests/serviceDispatchCallbacks.test.ts` now asserts the actionable field so a future reason cannot ship without it.
