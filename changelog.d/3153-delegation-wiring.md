### Added

- **Delegation live wiring + approval-record binding (#3153, #945 Q8):** the agent-access gate now enforces delegation budgets at invocation (a delegation-backed allow persists the delegation's incremented action count alongside the grant's; a spent delegation denies with BUDGET_EXHAUSTED), validates an attached settlement approval against its stored record (same hash, same version, same tenant, action granted, unexpired, unrevoked — a material proposal change after approval denies with APPROVAL_INVALID, an unresolvable approval with APPROVAL_UNKNOWN), and re-checks delegation validity at grant creation (revoked/expired delegations back no new grant). Approval records carry a required proposalVersion, validated alongside the hash. New self-service delegation routes: POST /api/iaaa/delegations/create (PDP-checked), GET /api/iaaa/delegations, POST /api/iaaa/delegations/revoke (delegator-only, with grant cascade). KV stores for delegations and approvals follow the existing agent-access adapter pattern. maxCost stays a future requirement (action-count only); the global IAAA_AGENT_ACCESS_MODE switch is unchanged by this slice.
