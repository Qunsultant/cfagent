### Added

- **Auction orders (MOO / MOC / LOO / LOC) on Alpaca, through the time-in-force the ticket already had (Qunsultant/cfagent#4671):** the order-type registry declared a `session_timed` capability that **no broker** could serve, and `opg`/`cls` existed only as unused words in a type. An auction order is not a fifth order type, though — it is a market or limit order whose time-in-force names an exchange auction, which is exactly how the broker models it. So `OPG` (at the open) and `CLS` (at the close) join the **one** time-in-force vocabulary: `market + opg` is a MOO, `market + cls` a MOC, `limit + opg` a LOO, `limit + cls` a LOC, derived from the pair rather than kept in a second table. The ticket offers them only where `config/order-capabilities.json` lists them (**Alpaca equity**, paper and live), the preview body and both execute routes carry them, and the `alpaca_place_order` tool schema offers them. E*TRADE declares no auction term, so it **refuses by name** at its seam — through the same registry entry — instead of quietly placing a day order that only looked session-timed; Public keeps its unverified-schema refusal. `describeAdvancedOrderTypes` now reports the capability as available on Alpaca and refused on E*TRADE.
