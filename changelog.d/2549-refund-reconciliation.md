### Refund doctrine reconciled: automatic credit AND operator-initiated cash refunds, XOR per failure (#2549)

- Owner decision (2026-10-31): both compensation mechanisms stand. The automatic failure compensation for a paid run remains a one-time replacement-run credit (deduped, never cash); cash refunds remain the operator-initiated path (#2204) — requested, operator-approved, operator-executed, audited, never automatic.
- Both gates' disclosures (`PAYMENT_DISCLOSURE`, `NATIVE_PAYMENT_DISCLOSURE`), the refund policy copy, both ledger credit memos, the payment terminal notes, the gate registry role, and the checkout/assurance UI copy now describe both mechanisms truthfully; the previous credit-only claims ("Automated refunds are not available", "never a cash refund", "cfpay cannot refund") are gone.
- Double compensation is now closed at the money gate: a run that already received its automatic failure credit could previously also be cash-refunded once its payment-intent id became resolvable. `executeRefund` now consults an injected failure-credit lookup before any gateway call — a credited run is refused (credit XOR refund per failure), an unwired lookup refuses pay-per-run execution, and a failing lookup refuses too. The route wires the lookup to both ledgers (native D1 credit rows and cfpay `mas_transactions` credit rows).
- The executed pay-per-run refund record now states its entitlement consequences explicitly, including `failure_credit_issued:yes|no|unchecked` alongside `run_already_delivered`.
- No amounts, prices, flags, networks, or compensation/refund mechanics changed. New boundary pins: `tests/refund_reconciliation_2549.test.ts` (disclosure truthfulness, automatic-path credit-only behavior + structural no-refund-capability pin, XOR at service and route level, operator-only execution, executed-refund audit).
