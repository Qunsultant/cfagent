### Added

- **Stock screening is now broker-neutral (#3272 S2):** `/api/etrade/screen` requires an E*TRADE login and gathers its universe through the E*TRADE client, so a tenant on any other broker could not screen stocks — although the screening math (`DynamicMarketScreener.screenWithQuotes`) was already pure. New `src/trading/stockQuoteSource.ts` is the missing interface: `StockQuoteSource` (`getQuote(symbol)`), `platformQuoteSource` to adapt any broker, `gatherQuotes` with bounded concurrency and **per-symbol** failure capture, and `screenSymbolsWithSource`, which runs the unchanged screener over those quotes. New route `POST /api/broker/screen` screens the **selected** broker's quotes for an explicit universe (max 25 symbols — each is one upstream read), returns `quotesRead`, names every symbol that could not be quoted in `failures`, refuses an empty universe with `400 invalid_request`, and answers `501 provider_unsupported` naming the broker when it has no quote read (before constructing it). The provider catalogue is deliberately untouched: `SCREENING_PROVIDER_DESCRIPTORS` is pinned to exactly the providers the default registry builds, so a descriptor entry must land together with a registered provider (next slice), not before it. The async workflow screen, the E*TRADE `listings` branch and universe discovery are deliberately out of scope: they are different data paths, not quote gathers. #ceo-aimp
