### Added

- **Alpaca option chains are readable by the copilot (#4014):** the broker could already assemble a chain (contracts + snapshots, #3233), but the only way to *read* one was the options HTTP routes — which sit behind the tenant data-provider capability gate and refuse when the selected provider does not declare `optionsChains` (Alpaca itself does not). A tenant on an Alpaca data provider therefore had no chain at all, and a multi-leg strategy (PMCC: buy a far-dated call, sell a near-dated call) could not be priced. New tool `alpaca_get_option_chain` (`mcp:read`) reads the broker's own chain — expiry dates plus contracts with bid/ask, last price, greeks and IV when Alpaca sends them — and picks the nearest expiry when none is named. Live-only and honest: an empty or absent chain throws an explicit unavailable error, a contract Alpaca cannot price stays omitted, and nothing is substituted from another provider. Pinned by `tests/alpaca_option_chain_tool_4014.test.ts` (chain read + expiry selection + audit, empty-chain refusal, platform without chain reads named).
