### Added
- Scheduled stock screens follow the same payment path as scheduled goal screens, as a payment decision at the tick (#2373 slice C): under monetization ON, a scheduled stock tick resolves the tenant, reads the APPROVED stock price from the operator price catalogue (fail-closed on missing/invalid/unapproved prices, per #2547), resolves the account, and — for covered runs — consumes subscription included usage or credits first via the payment workflow's own consume ports, then runs the chain/monolithic path exactly as today. Uncovered recurring runs skip, audited as stock_screen.skipped_uncovered and explained, never charged unattended; uncovered one-off runs skip with an honest explanation because one-off checkout is unavailable for stock schedules (the chain/monolithic run cannot ride the payment workflow, and the G7-rejected migration is not re-proposed). The schedule-creation guard rejects one_off stock schedules (with or without claimed authorization) and stamps absent scheduleKind as "recurring". Flag OFF behaviour is unchanged. The goal tick's price read is now approval-aware (getApprovedPrice), so an unapproved goal price fails closed to run_failed instead of routing to the payment workflow.
