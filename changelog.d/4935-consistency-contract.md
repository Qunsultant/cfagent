### Cross-store consistency contract: DO + D1 dual-write discipline (#4935)

Adds ADR 0033 (`docs/architecture/adr/0033-cross-store-consistency-contract.md`): idempotency rules, projection-lag tolerance per carrier, authoritative-vs-projection read rules, dual-write ordering (DO first), read-your-writes behaviour, and the decided non-goal of any cross-store atomicity mechanism with its grounds — plus the frozen audit ledger of every existing dual-write/projection path (five violations found, filed as follow-ups #4961–#4965). Adds `tests/cross_store_consistency_pin_4935.test.ts` to the `test:pins` suite: the dual-write call-site census, the DO-first ordering, the never-throw discipline, the reconcile operator-only rule, and the statement-level idempotency guard (with the F-2/F-3 exceptions allowlisted until #4962/#4963 land).
