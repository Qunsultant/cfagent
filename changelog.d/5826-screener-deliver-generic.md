### Added

- **Generic deliver carries screener delivery (#5826, #4437 slice 5):** `deliver.apply` gains a screener application (params `screener: true`) that delivers a screen outcome through the orchestrator sink — the same door the bespoke `screener.deliver` step calls (`deliverWorkflowScreenOutcome` for market outcomes, `deliverWorkflowGoalScreenOutcome` for goal outcomes), with the same `workflow:<jobId>` delivery id, goal/delivery/stage-chain threading, and canonical report id. The bespoke deliver rules carry over: a scheduled duplicate passes through untouched, `deliver: false` skips, a sink refusal is recorded (never a failed run), an unwired sink refuses `DELIVER_SCREENER_SINK_NOT_CONFIGURED` by name, and the screener application never consults the channel stores. The screener definition still names `screener.deliver` — the redefinition (slice 6, #5827) moves its deliver node onto this step and wires the sink in the residencies. Parity pinned by `tests/screener_deliver_generic_5826.test.ts` (10 tests). The deliver extension is recorded in `docs/architecture/agentic/j-stack-workflow-engine.md` §1.2. #CAIO-quns
