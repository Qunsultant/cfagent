### Changed

- **Module logging + coded messages across the platform families (#5607):** the G-5/G-6 gap is closed for the tenant, account, channel, and payment-assurance route modules. Every route logs entry, success-outcome, and refusal events through its module logger on the #3515 structured-logging seam (plus warn events for the previously silent degrade paths: operator-list, catalogue, identity-resolve, audit-annotation, instance-status, and signup-attribution failures), and every enumerated uncoded error literal is now a catalog-coded message. The catalog gains 7 codes: `FAILURE_COMMERCIAL_UNAUTHORIZED` (401), `FAILURE_COMMERCIAL_METHOD_NOT_ALLOWED` (405), `FAILURE_COMMERCIAL_OPERATOR_REQUIRED` (403), `FAILURE_PAYMENT_ASSURANCE_DISABLED` (403), `FAILURE_PAYMENT_ASSURANCE_NOT_ALLOWED` (403), `FAILURE_PAYMENT_ASSURANCE_UNAVAILABLE` (409), and `FAILURE_PAYMENT_ASSURANCE_LIVE_TEST_ONLY` (403). All statuses are unchanged; no endpoint, migration, or UX change. Out of scope (filed as #5851 under #5607): the remaining G-5/G-6 coverage in `platformUsers.ts`, `adminTenantQueues.ts`, `agenticPayments.ts`, `premium.ts`, `meter.ts`, `adsRevenueReferrals.ts`, and `audit.ts`.
