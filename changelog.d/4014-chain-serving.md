### Fixed

- **Options chains are served by a capable provider instead of refusing (#4014):** every live-chain options route (`/etrade/options/chains`, `/expire-dates`, `/strategy`, `/trading/options/recommend|llm-ideas|compare|comparison|strategies|best-trade|screen|unified`) refused with `FAILURE_DATA_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` whenever the tenant's selected data provider did not declare `optionsChains` — which is the case for Alpaca, Yahoo and Nasdaq. A tenant on an Alpaca data provider therefore had **no chain at all** on the screens, even though a capable provider (E*TRADE) was enabled: the PMCC incident. Chains are now served by a capable provider, and the run record names both — `dataProviderId` is the provider that served, `requestedDataProviderId` keeps the tenant's selection visible — so no chain is ever labelled with a provider that did not serve it. Pinned by `tests/options_chain_serving_4014.test.ts` and the updated gate pins in `tests/options_provider_gate.test.ts`.
