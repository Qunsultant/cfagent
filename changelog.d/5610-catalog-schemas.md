### Changed

- **Catalog extension + shared request/response schemas (#5610):** the 10 parameterized commercial routes (Group C) and the 19 platform routes (accounts, tenants, channels, users) now match through the URL catalogs (`src/data/commercialUrlCatalog.ts`, new `src/data/platformUrlCatalog.ts`) instead of duplicated path literals — handlers, clients, and BizCopilot call sites all build paths from the catalogs. A shared request/response schemas registry (`src/api/commercialSchemas.ts`, 120 operations) covers every catalog operation, reusing the #5604 shape schemas where they exist, and the ONE OpenAPI spec (`src/security/openapiSpec.ts`) generates its CBO path items from the registry — the hand-written `payments-agentic` wildcard path item is removed (the `premium` wildcard stays, out of scope). No live behavior changes: routing, methods, statuses, and decode rules are unchanged; the registry is the spec + pin contract in this slice and is NOT wired into live route validation (follow-up sub-issue under #5610).
