### Added

- **Gate → native-ledger wiring (#2192 follow-up, #2146):** after a successful native charge confirmation in the payment workflow, the flow best-effort records one native D1 ledger charge row via the native ledger port (tenant-scoped from the verified flow params, truthful gateway per rail, idempotent on replay via the port's per-run dedupe). Any ledger failure is loud-logged and never thrown into the payment path, so the payment outcome is identical with the wiring on or off, except for the recorded row. Covered runs, cfpay-era confirmations, and unconfirmed payments record no native row. No charge/capture/confirm semantic, amount, refund, check-payment, or assurance change; the charge journal projection already posted by the adapter's payment.confirmed handler is unchanged (no second journal wiring added).
