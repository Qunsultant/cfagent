### Added

- **Workflow catalog: the typed declaration of what can be run, and what each stage is (#3071, #3069 S1):** `src/workflows/workflowCatalog.ts` declares every workflow the platform can run — stock screener, options screener, trading leg — with, per stage, its label key, what it persists, the stage-message topic it publishes, the previous stage's artifact it consumes, all four mode axes (timing, concurrency, volume, partitioning), its `pipelinePolicy` retry bucket, its checkpoint flag and its approval requirement. `assertWorkflowCatalog` refuses a duplicate id, an empty or out-of-order stage list, an undeclared stage name, and any stage missing a mode axis — so a half-declared workflow cannot reach the UI. Stock is declared runnable with its six stages in canonical producer→consumer order; the trading leg carries its approval gate and is declared not-runnable. 12 pins. This is the declaration the catalog UI (#3074) and the stage control plane (#3069) read; it adds no runtime behaviour.
