### Added
- Journal recognition schedule (#2155 follow-up): new pure module `src/services/journalRecognition.ts` computing the straight-line daily schedule that moves deferred revenue (2100) to revenue (4000) over a subscription period, derived from the tenant's own deferred entries + subscription periods (integer-cent split, so installments sum exactly to the deferred amount), plus `postDueRecognition` / `postDueRecognitionBestEffort` posting due installments as balanced Dr 2100 / Cr 4000 pairs, idempotent on `${purchaseRef}:recognition:${n}` transactionRefs, tenant-scoped with cross-tenant failure closed, and best-effort (never thrown into a payment path). No autonomous sweeper, no cron, no HTTP route, and no wiring into any payment path is added here — nothing runs on its own until a future slice wires the posting function to an explicit caller; the JournalPort shape is unchanged, so a future real-GL adapter still swaps in behind the same port.
