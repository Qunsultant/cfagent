### Added

- Webhook-triggered scheduling (#3311): `POST /api/schedules/webhook/followup` lets a workflow step call an authenticated, PDP-authorized webhook endpoint and the scheduler mints a scheduled follow-up job/sub-job (followup.report / deliver / notify) on the async-job chain, carrying the §4 correlation ID (trigger, scheduleId, asyncJobId, workflowId). Both the webhook call and the mint are audited (`schedule.webhook.received`, `schedule.webhook.followup_minted`). The webhook consumes #3122's capability allow-list, PDP enforcement (task:create), audit, tenant scope (per-login instance), and no-self-re-arm — not the agent proposal gate (the caller is a workflow executing the owner's already-authorized pipeline). Unauthenticated / unauthorized calls fail closed and are audited; the follow-up fires under the chain's own guards.
