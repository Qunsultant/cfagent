### Added

- **Broker-neutral options reads now have an HTTP surface (#3233):** the options data plane existed behind the platform seam, but no route could reach it for a broker other than E*TRADE — `/etrade/options/*` is owned by the E*TRADE options module and cannot grow conditions (its inventory is byte-pinned by #995). `src/agents/routes/brokerOptions.ts` adds an additive neutral surface, `GET|POST /api/broker/options/chains` and `GET|POST /api/broker/options/expire-dates`, served by the **selected** broker. A capability the broker does not implement (catalog-declared or absent at runtime) is answered `501 provider_unsupported` naming that broker — never an empty list, never another broker's data, never a silent E*TRADE fallback. A broker that answers with nothing quotable is reported as explicit absence (`chain: null` + message), not an empty success. The persisted trading environment (`etrade:environment`, #2482) is echoed so the response names which environment and broker produced it. `tests/broker_options_routes_3233.test.ts` pins all four lies; `tests/thin_router_inventory.test.ts` records the two new conditions in the #359 route inventory. #ceo-aimp
