### Changed

- Full scheduling flexibility with granularity first (Qunsultant/cfagent#4235): any valid 5-field cron is accepted and `isDue` honors all five fields (the old no-timeZone step-hour never-fires quirk is gone — a step-hour cron now fires on its step hours, and a monthly cron fires monthly, not daily); any interval is accepted. The default is at most once per day — a sub-daily cadence needs an explicit per-schedule opt-in (`subDailyOptIn`), recorded on the goal/schedule at creation and update, enforced in `addGoal`, `resolveScheduleUpdate`, and POST /api/schedules/create, and shown in the create form and the cadence editor when the current cadence needs it. The UX offers monthly, semi-annual, and annual cron presets plus bi-weekly, 12-hour, and 24-hour interval presets, with free cron/interval input uncapped. A legacy sub-daily schedule without a recorded opt-in fires at most daily until its owner re-opts it in via update.
