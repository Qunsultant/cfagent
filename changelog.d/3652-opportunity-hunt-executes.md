### Fixed

- **The `opportunity-hunt` goal now runs instead of failing every firing (#3652):** `search.opportunity_hunt` was absent from `OPERATION_CATALOG`, so `createJob` threw `Unknown operation: search.opportunity_hunt` on every due tick and the goal recorded a failure run while staying due. The operation is now registered on the search agent, declared agent-schedulable (it executes at firing), and implemented in `src/services/opportunityHunt.ts`: it scans the persisted technicals closes with the same `evaluateLatestPatternSignal` evaluator the Stock Copilot plan step uses, records `N setup(s) across M symbol(s)` on the run row, and fails with a named reason (`opportunity_hunt_no_bars`, `opportunity_hunt_store_unbound`, `opportunity_hunt_bar_source_failed`) rather than reporting an empty success when the bar source is unreadable. A hunt that finds nothing is a completed hunt, not a failure. #ceo-aimp
