### Added

- **Product Hub: exact links for the Admin Products sub-tabs (#4689):** the Admin Products tab's three sub-tabs are URL-synced on a nested `adminTab` param, so every product-lifecycle stage has an exact link — Products at `?tab=product-hub&view=admin-products`, Create product (add a product) at `?tab=product-hub&view=admin-products&adminTab=create-product`, and Create version (add another price — a new version is a new price) at `?tab=product-hub&view=admin-products&adminTab=create-version`. Both new addresses are deep links in the Product Hub sub-menu. The nested param does not leak across hub tabs: the hub strips `adminTab` when the view moves away from admin-products, and the section resets to Products while hidden. No change to the selling/checkout/refund flows, access rules, or the outcome catalog.
