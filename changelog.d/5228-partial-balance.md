### Added

- Partial referral-balance conversion (#5228, sub-issue of #2405): a vested referral balance below a run's price now converts as a partial discount — the balance tops up the commercial account and the remaining price is charged through the usual payment path (x402 / included runs / credits, existing order). A balance at or above the price converts exactly as before; a zero balance converts nothing. Conversion goes through the single balance-read path (`LifecycleStore.redeemableBalance`) and the existing `convertReferralCreditsForRun`, so the follow-up expiry slice (2.31) plugs its filter into the same path. Run prices only — referral credits still cannot buy subscriptions (owner decision on 2.25). No new `paidVia` values, no new outcome codes, no earn/vest rule change, no price change.
