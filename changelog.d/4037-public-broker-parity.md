### Added

- **Public.com's broker surface is pinned at parity, including what it deliberately lacks (#4037):** the platform implements every required method (status, quote, accounts, positions, preview, execute, place, cancel, order history, chains, expiries) — the surface itself landed with #3990; what was missing was the contract that says so. New pins assert the required methods exist, that the optional gaps are **absent rather than stubbed** (`getPositionLots`, `supportsSessionOrders`, watchlists → the neutral routes answer `provider_unsupported`), that TEST placement refuses with `FAILURE_PUBLIC_NO_PAPER_MODE` **before any broker call** (the fetch is never invoked), and that a placement carrying `status: NEW` is reported `submitted` while only a `FILLED` payload is `executed` — an acceptance receipt is never presented as a fill.
