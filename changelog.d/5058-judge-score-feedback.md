### Added

- **Judge quality scores now have a consumer (#5058, AG-4):** every chat turn was scored by `LLMJudge.evaluate` and the score landed only in the `response.evaluated` audit event and a best-effort VectorMemory row, so the evaluation loop was audit-only. A turn scored below `JUDGE_LEARNING_THRESHOLD` (default 60/100) now becomes a `judge`-domain learning in `mas_learnings` — deduped per request id — and the weekly reflection job aggregates the recent rows (`summarizeJudgeScoreLearnings`) into a review finding once `JUDGE_LEARNING_MIN_COUNT` low scores land inside `JUDGE_LEARNING_WINDOW_DAYS`, naming the agents and the mean score. The write is best-effort by construction (a learning-store failure never fails the chat turn), every threshold is externalized (`JUDGE_LEARNING_ENABLED`, `_THRESHOLD`, `_MIN_COUNT`, `_WINDOW_DAYS`, declared on `Env` and documented in `.dev.vars.example`), and `runReflection` now takes the deployment env so the operator's thresholds — not the defaults — decide. Pinned by `tests/judge_score_feedback_5058.test.ts`. #clo-arsi
