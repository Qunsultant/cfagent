### Fixed

- **Stale-async-job reap no longer kills live, heartbeating jobs (#3907):** the 2h reap in `sweepStaleAsyncJobs` and the `getAsyncJob` read-path reaped on wall-clock age alone and never looked at the heartbeat the executor maintains every 2 minutes — on 2026-11-09 it killed two live paid MCP jobs (`paid_options_screener`, `paid_market_research`) in one sweep tick after 2h11m, after their x402 charges were taken and with no result. The reap now consults the heartbeat: a row is reaped only past 2h AND with a stale heartbeat (`last_activity_at` older than 3 × the heartbeat interval); pre-heartbeat rows fall back to `started_at` age. `executeAsyncJob` races every capability leg against a configurable max-runtime deadline (default 90 min, env `ASYNC_JOB_MAX_RUNTIME_MINUTES`) that fails with the new cataloged code `FAILURE_ASYNC_JOB_TIMEOUT`; `onStart` fails fast orphaned `running` rows with stale heartbeats (never re-runs them); paid commands audit `agentic_payment.tool_paid_started` before the long call; `retryAsyncJob` refuses paid payloads whose signature was already consumed; and the Queues page reports honestly (state-counted pill, "Showing 5 of N", DLQ header reads "Could not load" on failure, DLQ routes always return `{error}` JSON). No refund/credit mechanism exists yet — filed as #3908; provider-level fetch timeouts filed as #3909. #clo-arsi
