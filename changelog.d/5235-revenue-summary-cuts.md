### Added

- Subscription + pay-per-run revenue in the revenue summary (#5235, sub-issue of #2405, Goal 4): `get_revenue_summary` / `GET /revenue` / the Revenue tab now carry subscription revenue and count, pay-per-run revenue by screener family (stock / options / goal / strategy, zero-filled, unknown bucket when present), and served runs by coverage type (paid / subscription_included / credits / unknown, with list-price value), all aggregated from the ledger of record (`mas_transactions`) plus the metering store of record (`usage_events`) — no second ledger. Subscription purchases now write a best-effort receipt row at activation (`tx_bill-<checkoutRequestId>`, gateway stripe, bundle precedent), so the subscription line has a source; pre-slice purchases have no row and renewals are not recorded in the ledger (no webhook ledger write) — both documented, never estimated. Cost per run is not recorded anywhere (verified: `rolloutBudget` holds only caller-supplied budget/actual and measures nothing), so `costPerRun` is null and the tab shows "Unavailable" — never an estimate. The ads / marketplace / fees / payouts lines and the gross/net formulas are unchanged; the new lines are additive and are not folded into gross or net. No new config values, no new outcome codes, no price/tier/entitlement/billing change; testnet only.
