### Added

- Free-run counter (#3305, sub-issue of #3241): the decided Free allowance — 10 runs per product per UTC calendar month, operator-managed and changeable — is now real in the native gate. A per-account, per-product, per-month counter in D1 (migration 0043, port-first) lets a Free-tier account run up to the allowance without payment (price 0, no checkout, no credit spend); at the cap the gate falls through to the existing at-limit behavior (402 for manual runs, skip for scheduled runs). The allowance is operator data seeded at 10 and changeable without code; the month boundary is the UTC calendar month. An unlimited-free grant bypasses the counter, trial/Subscribed runs use included runs (never the counter), and no counter wired means today's behavior (no free runs). No price, flag, or secret change.
