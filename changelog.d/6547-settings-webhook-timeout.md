### Fixed

- **The settings-change webhook can no longer hold the OrchestratorAgent resident (#6547):** the notifier delivers through the instance's own webhook channel, deferred through the DO context's `waitUntil`. Its fetch had no timeout and no abort signal, so a hung self-delivery left the promise unsettled — and because the leg never throws by contract, the residency was silent rather than an error (a pending I/O operation can hold a Durable Object in memory for up to 15 minutes). The delivery is now bounded by `SETTINGS_WEBHOOK_TIMEOUT_MS` (default 10s, clamped 1ms–60s through the one resolver `resolveSettingsWebhookTimeoutMs`), carried as an `AbortSignal.timeout` on the request; a delivery that times out is reported as not-delivered with the bound named in the log, never thrown into the write path. Pins: `tests/settings_webhook_timeout_6547.test.ts` (a hung delivery settles in the bound, the request carries the signal, the bound is configuration and clamped, a healthy delivery is unchanged). The universal-resolver census gains the one CANONICAL row deliberately, with its reason recorded in the pin and the PR.
