### Added

- **Feed-run residency decision + scheduled-silence accountability (#3992 PR-5):** the closing slice of the #3976 repair records, with evidence, that production pipeline/feed runs STAY resident in the ScheduledAgent host (`docs/architecture/feed-run-residency-and-scheduled-silence-3992.md`). The JobPipelineWorkflow residency remains provisioned and uncalled — a source scan proves `startPipelineJobWorkflow` has no production caller outside its definition and its Durable Object door — and activation, if ever justified, happens only under the follow-up issue "Workflow-residency activation for pipeline runs" (sub-issue of #3992), whose chief prerequisite is closing the one remaining boundary: the workflow deps builder omits the engine's `runSettledObserver`, so a workflow-resident feed run would settle with no Job outcomes row. Moving buys durable step-resume for executions now bounded at 240s whole-run (`FEED_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED`, PR-4), while host-eviction losses are already settled and named by #4002's orphan settlement on host start; the #3992 bounds themselves are residency-independent (shared engine and step registry, catalogue default in both deps builders). The scheduled silence is named with its mechanism and its limit: the firing path (declarations from `ScheduledAgent.getScheduledTasks`, dispatch voided at the declaration boundary by design, the run's records the only failure surface) admits three candidate mechanisms for the Nov 9 09:35 → Nov 10 window — declarations absent on the then-live host, firings failing before any record existed on the swallowed pre-#3976 hydration path (removed by #3979, which moved hydration into the runners, where every failure records by name), or the host never woken — and code cannot discriminate among them for the historical window. Going forward the silence is self-naming: declarations skipped (warn log), orphan settles, leg timeouts, and the deadline settle each leave a record, so a recurrence of unexplained silence is a new incident, not a reopened thread. No behaviour change: the decision is pinned by `tests/feed_residency_decision_3992.test.ts` (doc sections + decision content, the no-production-caller invariant, and the onStart orphan-settle wiring), so a future activation fails the suite until the decision doc is amended in the same PR.
