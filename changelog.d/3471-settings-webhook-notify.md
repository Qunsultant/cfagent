### Added

- **Stock Copilot push leg: selection changes notify the copilot through the built-in webhook channel (#3471, on #3474's foundation):** when a user changes their trading mode (environment switch) or their broker selection (tenant providers), the committed write now delivers a signed webhook to the user's own OrchestratorAgent instance at its reserved `/webhooks/settings` path — in-Worker, through the instance's DO stub fetch, signed with the port's own HMAC seam (the signing side now sits beside the #3474 verifier in `thinkPortMapping.ts`). The payload carries the FACT of change only (kind, login, changedAt): the orchestrator's new `onWebhook` hook re-resolves the effective selection through the #3451 resolver (the stores stay authoritative), compares it with the selection held in the instance's Agent state, and on a real change writes a "selection changed" entry (old → new, source: notified — new `selection_changed` activity outcome, rendered by the copilot panel) into the activity feed and `setState`s the new selection, whose base-Agent broadcast is the push #3472's panel leg consumes. No change ⇒ silence; an event naming another login is ignored; the first notification establishes the held baseline. Delivery fails open to the #3473 observe floor by design: with `THINK_WEBHOOK_SECRET` unset the delivery is skipped with a loud log (never sent unsigned) and a failed delivery never fails the user's settings write. #CAIO-quns
