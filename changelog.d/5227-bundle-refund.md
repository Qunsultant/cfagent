### Added

- Bundle refunds (#5227, sub-issue of #2405, Goal 3): bundles are refundable under the decided policy — a 30-day window from purchase (the operator refund-policy row, never hardcoded) and proration by the unused remainder, computed by the policy from the remaining entitlement period (bundle run-consumption tracking does not exist yet, so the remaining-period fraction is the proration basis). The amount is computed, not chosen: a supplied amount that differs from the computed remainder is refused, and a fully-used bundle has nothing refundable. The payment intent resolves from the purchase row's stored id, else the provider subscription's first invoice (earliest invoice wins; a renewal's intent is never returned); an unresolvable bundle is refused at request time, never queued. Executing a bundle refund revokes the purchase and its entitlements immediately (remaining access ends with the unused remainder it refunds). The "bundle" kind is approved through the existing refund-policy UX (RefundPolicySection) and requested through the existing Billing-tab refund flow (RefundFlowSection, amount left blank); the retired FAILURE_BUNDLE_REFUND_UNDECIDED code stays in the outcome catalog (append-only) and is no longer emitted. No change to the subscription or pay-per-run refund kinds, windows, or their allowPartial setting; cfpay refunds remain out of scope by design.
