### Added

- **Unified Jobs UI (#2964):** Platform Admin → Jobs, beside Scheduled goals: one surface listing ScheduledAgent goals and Orchestrator durable schedules with per-run history and latest-result detail, plus Worker cron triggers read-only and labelled deployment-only. Per-run result store: every goal firing that reaches a terminal point and every firing of the four creatable orchestrator callbacks leaves a stored record (system, job id, operation/callback, started/finished, success/failure/skipped outcome, error text on failure, skip reason on skipped, descriptor and artifact refs on success); manual triggers of non-creatable callbacks via the generic trigger route are recorded invocation-level. New endpoints `GET /api/schedule/runs`, `GET /api/schedule/systems`, `GET /api/schedules/runs`, `GET /api/schedules/:id/runs`, `GET /api/schedules/callbacks`; goal and schedule creation/triggering reuse the existing endpoints. No existing panel, endpoint, or store removed; the stores are not merged. Platform firings of internal-only callbacks have no per-run record in this slice (carried). Pinned by `tests/job-run-records-2964.test.ts` (20 tests). #CISO-bhdm
