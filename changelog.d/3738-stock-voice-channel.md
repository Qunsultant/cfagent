### Added

- Stock Copilot voice channel (#3738): the Stock Copilot panel gains a voice control that runs turns through the shared voice-desk client (the Trading Hub voice tab's STT → voice turn → TTS machinery, extracted to `src/client/voiceDeskClient.ts` and consumed by both surfaces), carrying the stock copilot's context so the #3720 desk composition serves it. Channel enablement for every copilot registration is now an externalized switch (new `src/services/copilotChannels.ts`) resolved stored setting > `COPILOT_CHANNELS` wrangler var > code default with the deciding layer named; production sets `stock:voice=on` in the env layer. A voice turn arriving while its channel switch is off is refused by name (`COPILOT_CHANNEL_DISABLED`) and audited, and the copilot control route persists per-user channel decisions to the settings store.
