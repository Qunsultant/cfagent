### Added

- Grant-rule mechanics for #945 Q9 (Qunsultant/cfagent#3155, LIVE per the 2026-11-04 full-enablement directive): tenant-admin and platform-admin grants now require two distinct approvers — the first admin's grant proposes (202, pending proposal) and only a second, distinct admin's identical grant mints it; non-admin grants mint immediately as before. Tenant-scope grants without an explicit expiry now carry a configurable default lifetime of 90 days (`IAAA_GRANT_DEFAULT_EXPIRY_DAYS`; platform-scope bindings untouched, genesis grants exempt by construction since bootstrap bypasses `grantMembership`). A granter-review queue (`GET /api/iaaa/role-grants?review=true`) lists live grants whose granter is fully revoked — review, not cascade: grants survive granter revocation, except grants that violate the grant matrix on their face, which are revoked with the granter at revoke time. The membership grant, role-grants grant, membership edit, and access-request decision surfaces all enforce the approver rule; the admin panels show an awaiting-second-approval notice instead of claiming success.
