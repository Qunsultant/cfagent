### Fixed

- **The introspection surface reports the universe the cycle runs (#4027):** `introspectStockCopilot` resolved the **sync** preset, so a copilot configured with an index preset described a hand-written subset (or a bare preset name) while the cycle scanned the stored membership — the same defect #3936 fixed on the status surface, left behind because the introspection deps carried no SQL executor. The deps now accept `sql` (passed by both call sites); with it, the universe comes from `resolveCopilotUniverseFull` and the configuration block reports `universeSource`, `universeSnapshotDate` and `universeFallbackReason` alongside the preset. Without a store the read is unchanged. Pinned by `tests/introspection_universe_4027.test.ts` (stored membership + snapshot, labelled fallback, no-SQL fallback to the sync read).
