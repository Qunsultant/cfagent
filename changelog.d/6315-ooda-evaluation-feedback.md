### Changed

- **A research run now states what the evaluation history did — and why it could not reorder anything (#773 (a), sub-issue #6315):** every report carries `evaluationFeedback` — the source, how many ideas were published and evaluated, the per-family slices (`evaluated`, `winRate`, `avgRMultiple`, `directionAccuracyRecorded`), the 10-evaluation bar, whether the order actually changed, and one sentence an operator can read. Three honest states are pinned: no evaluations ("the loop has nothing to feed back"), evaluations without a per-family direction accuracy ("the structure order was not changed by history … win rates are recorded and shown; they are not yet an ordering input"), and a family at the bar. **No decision changes** — the ranked structures are byte-identical to before.

  The verification behind it found the reorder inert: `researchPipeline.ts` reads `(slice as any).directionAccuracy`, but `TrackRecordSlice` has no such field (`researchTrackRecord.ts:50-57`), so the accuracy map was always empty and the landed "structure ordering by historical accuracy" half never ran — while every report read as if it had. The report now says so; #773 (b) either records the accuracy on the slice or names win rate as the ordering input. #COO-spr9
