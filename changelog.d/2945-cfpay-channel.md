### Added

- cfpay as a separate pay-per-use channel (#2945, monetization Phase 2.17): one listed catalogue product per purchase, sold through the Products-panel Selling section only (no second purchase path). The payments-agent card takes no amount input, so the channel sells only products whose APPROVED catalogue price exactly equals the operator-attested channel fixed price (new billing-policy field `cfpayChannelFixedPriceUSD`, null = channel unavailable; mint refuses 409 on mismatch, 503 when unattested). Confirm is the HITL step mirroring 2.1 Billing's "I have paid": checkout.status is retrieved and only an allowlist paid status (paid/completed/fulfilled/succeeded) with matching requestId/email grants credit — exactly the bound price via the existing commercial credits — plus a `mas_transactions` row (gateway `cfpay-channel`) so the existing receipts route surfaces it, and an operator-only totals-level reconcile (match/mismatch/unverifiable; record-level matching is not claimed since the card publishes no record schemas). Refunds go through the 2.7 machinery with the new `cfpay-channel` rail and unchanged per-kind operator approval; execution refuses with the existing not-resolvable 422 while no payment intent is resolvable (the card cannot move funds or refund). No subscription via the channel, no new ledger, no amount ever read from a cfpay field, testnet-only, money routes flag-gated.
