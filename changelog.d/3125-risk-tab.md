### Trading

- Risk analysis is now a tab on the Fast Order Ticket page and analyzes any security type: the order page gains a Risk tab seeded from the live ticket (an equity ticket seeds Long Equity, an options ticket seeds its legs as a multi-leg subject), the standalone page gains a Stock/Options switch with a legs editor, and the Fast Order Ticket "Risk Analysis" sub-menu now lands on the Risk tab. Composition needs no component changes: the Risk tab renders the same RiskAnalysisPanel the standalone route renders, with chrome applied by the host (PageChromeContext page/tab/nested), and the panel is rebuilt to the order-workspace pattern (form card, summary card, responsive grid, a card per block). Ticket legs carry no IV, so options Greeks from the tab are disclosed as partial with the missing-IV leg count, never silently complete.
