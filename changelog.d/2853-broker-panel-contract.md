### Added

- **Broker panel contract — the selected broker now decides what renders (#2853, gaps G2 / #2594):** the trading hub was written against one broker, so an Alpaca tenant saw E*TRADE panels: portfolio, positions, orders, diagnostics, options. The fix is a contract rather than a second page. `src/trading/brokerPanels.ts` answers, per `(broker, surface)` — accounts, positions, orders, options — whether the surface may render, derived from `BROKER_CATALOG` (#3232) rather than a second hand-written table. A refusal carries the code the server sends (`FAILURE_BROKER_UNAVAILABLE`), names the broker and the missing capability, and states that no other broker's data is substituted. `src/client/BrokerSurfaceGate.tsx` is the only place that decision is rendered: the broker's surface, or the named notice — never an empty panel. `src/client/AlpacaBrokerPanel.tsx` is the first non-E*TRADE surface under the contract: Alpaca's accounts and positions with its own label and its trading type (paper/live from the persisted environment), built from `ui/` primitives only. The hub swaps `AccountPanels` for the Alpaca panel when the selection is not E*TRADE, and states plainly that portfolio-risk analytics are E*TRADE-only instead of rendering them under another broker's name. Two neutral reads back it — `GET /api/broker/accounts` and `GET /api/broker/positions` (`src/agents/routes/brokerReads.ts`), same honesty rules as the options reads: the selected broker serves the call, an unsupported capability is a coded 501 naming that broker, a failed read is never an empty list. Rollout is gated by a new `broker_panel_contract` flag (explicit OFF wins; absent inherits `options_screener` through `getFeatureFlagParent`, while `OPTIONS_PRODUCT_FLAG_PARENTS` keeps its #2596 meaning), and a disabled read answers `FEATURE_DISABLED` rather than rendering an empty panel. #cfo-qunso
