### Changed
- Chat turn submission in OrchestratorAgent is now durable (#2566): the `runAgenticChatTurn` driver submits turns through Think's SDK submission queue (`submitMessages`) instead of driving the turn facet in wait mode, so a turn submitted just before an instance restart still completes afterwards, and a re-submission under an explicit idempotency key collects the original outcome instead of executing a second turn. The POST /api/chat {reply, route} contract, per-turn identity carriage, the degraded-turn fallback, and CHAT_NLQ_MODE semantics are unchanged; decision recorded in ADR 0024.
