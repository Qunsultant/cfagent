### Added

- **Stock Copilot panel in the chat tab (#3422, parent #3391):** the paper-trading loop is no longer invisible — the chat view (`?tab=chat`) now carries a Stock Copilot panel showing the loop's live status (Live / Paused / Outside market hours / Off), its open positions with their exit plans (entry, stop, target, opened-at), recently closed positions with exit reasons, and a per-cycle activity feed. New routes in the orchestrator route family serve the signed-in user's own instance only (failing closed without a verified login): `GET /api/stock-copilot/status` and `POST /api/stock-copilot/control`. The control is a per-instance pause: paused cycles keep managing exits (stops, targets, strategy exits) but place no new entries, and resume restores entries from the next firing; the global `STOCK_COPILOT_PAPER_ENABLED` flag remains the master switch above it. Storage stays behind the data layer — the #3399 port family grows a closed-positions read, a bounded cycle-activity log (newest 100 per login), and the control record, with DO-SQLite and in-memory adapters; paused and outside-hours firings are not recorded as cycles. No new order paths: the only write is the pause/resume control. Performance measurement remains #3397. #CAIO-quns
