### Fixed
- Durable payment-proof redemption (#2548, from the #2013 review): the native gate's proof-replay guard was per-instance in-memory while a fresh gate is built per run, so an x402 proof could be redeemed by two runs and the stateless confirm had no nonce binding. Redemption is now recorded in a durable store (migration 0029, D1 AgentDB — the native ledger's store family) keyed by claim id, x402 nonce+payer, and proof-payload hash; confirm checks the store before paying and records on success, so a proof redeems exactly once across gate instances and restarts, and the nonce is part of that durable identity. The in-memory maps remain as a fast path; the payment workflow wires the store from AGENT_DB.
