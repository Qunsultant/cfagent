### Added
- Delegation Contract preview on the scheduled-goal creation form (Qunsultant/cfagent#1773, HAX #1 scope disclosure): before adding a goal, the form now shows what will be done, what will not be done, which systems will be accessed, which actions still require approval, budget and duration limits, and expected outputs, built purely from the form fields and the loaded operation-catalog entry (blank fields and unloaded catalog entries render as not-entered/not-loaded, never invented). The SessionStore resource-goals preset carries the same preview for its three fixed goals. Creating a goal authorizes its scheduled runs only; live trades, payments, and publishes still require their own approvals.
