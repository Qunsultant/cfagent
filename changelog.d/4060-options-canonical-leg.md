### Options canonical leg (#4060)

- The options `persistCanonicalResults` stage writes its canonical row through the engine's tenant-aware leg (`createTenantAwareCanonicalReportStore`, #3940) instead of the route's direct ORM write, in both residencies (workflow + host): the leg records "wired" (never `not_wired`) for an options report, and the canonical row lands in `mas_reports` where the Reports surface reads it.
- The `UnifiedOptionsRunRecord` shape rides the leg unchanged: one normalization (`unifiedOptionsRunRecordToStoreInput`) serves both the direct recorder and the leg adapter, and the manual record builder's title/source/generation-id scheme (`options_scanner:manual:<iso>` as the generation id, filter + result + provenance on the first write) maps onto the leg's input verbatim.
- The manual entry binds the leg (`createManualOptionsCanonicalLeg`, the workflow-residency construction) for both the persist stage and the shapeReport re-record — one seam instance, one transport for the row; the re-record backfills the report keys onto the same row via the leg.
- The goal leg's omitted-canonical case is decided explicitly and kept as an honest no-write (#783): the composing goal run's row stays the single canonical home, and the stage records `omitted` with the coded reason `options_canonical_omitted_goal_leg`, identically in both residencies. A leg refusal or throw records `failed` with its coded reason and never fails the run.
- The legacy direct store (`canonical`) is kept as a labeled transitional path (`direct`); no production caller passes it after this slice.
- The leg seam itself moved to a leaf: `CanonicalReportStoreSeam`, `PipelineGenerateReportDoor`, and `createTenantAwareCanonicalReportStore` now live in `src/services/canonicalReportLeg.ts` (type-only imports, no runtime graph) and are re-exported from `src/services/generateJobs.ts`, so every existing importer keeps its path. Importing the leg no longer drags in the generate job's graph (`analyzeJobs` → `enrichJobs` → `feedJobs` → `indexListJob`, whose module-scope `SECTOR_IDS` spread breaks under a mocked trading barrel — `tests/market_data_modes_1727.test.ts`).
