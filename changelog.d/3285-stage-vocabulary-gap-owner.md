### Changed

- **One stage vocabulary, and a named gap when there is no path (#3285):** the two vocabularies the issue reported are now one — the display stages ARE the contract's `STAGE_NAMES`, the options family's display stages partition its eight addressable ids, and the strategy family reuses the options list by design. The retired `not_implemented` stage state is gone, and the unit status set is the contract's closed union, so "`not_implemented` here, `unknown` there" cannot recur (pinned across the four stage surfaces). The residual half is now enforced in code: **a family that maps no runtime stage ids must declare a `runtimeMappingGap` that names its owning issue (`#NNNN`) and states a reason**, a gap note without either is refused at catalog validation, and a mapped family may not declare a gap at all. Today's catalog already satisfies it — the goal family names #3072 and the strategy family #3073, each with its reason. #autoclaw-001
