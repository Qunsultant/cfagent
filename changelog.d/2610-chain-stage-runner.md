### Changed

- Sharded chain advance decisions now run on the stage-runner contract (issue 2610): the chain builds one single-stage plan per stage (invocation async-alarm), projects the stage checkpoint from its shard rows, and decides through planNext/resumeUnits — the same runner, checkpoint store projection, planNext, and resume operation the engine already drives for manual, scheduled, and workflow runs. Submission construction (shard specs, stagger, parent linkage), the feed dependency order, the assemble gate, and the per-format options rules are unchanged; stock chains keep C3 stop semantics and options chains keep the per-format advance-past-failed-leg rule. A healed retry (failed row + completed retry row) reads completed; an unhealed failure still blocks; a symbol whose retry is queued is still waited on at feed assembly. The production checkpoint-store binding (createAsyncJobStageCheckpointStore) remains unwired — the bridge projects through the same checkpointFromStageRows projection the store wraps; binding the store into the alarm hooks is a follow-on seam.
