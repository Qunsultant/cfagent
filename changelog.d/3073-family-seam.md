### Added

- **The stage seam is no longer stock-only (#3073, #3069 S3/R2):** `StageMessage` now carries a `family` (`stock | options | trading | payment | saga`) and validates its `stageId` against that family's declared runtime ids — the same ids the workflow catalog renders, from the same dependency-free module, so a family cannot publish a stage the catalog does not know about. Stock keys keep their historical `run::stage` shape (claims written by running instances still match); other families are namespaced `run::family::stage`.
- **Payment publishes a boundary per completed step:** all sixteen `step.do` calls route through a `doStep` wrapper that emits after the durable step returns, so the artifact the message references (the step's own persisted record) always exists before the publish. A throwing emitter, a missing queue or a malformed id degrades to a logged line and never changes the payment's outcome — pinned by an equivalence test that compares a working seam against a broken one.
- **Saga publishes per declared stage and stays quiet for its waits:** the five declared stages route through `doStage`; `poll-<leg>-<round>` is a wait, not a stage, and publishes nothing — pinned in both directions.
- **Trading publishes at its two real steps:** the approval wait and the settle, in that order, with the boundary outside the settle's failure surface. The catalog's invented `approval/execute/fill/reconcile` list is gone from this path entirely.
