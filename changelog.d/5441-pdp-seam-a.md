### Security

- **IAAA PDP seam wired into live route handlers (#5441):** the platform-admin gates (IAAA overview, tenant queues, KV wipe, user administration, service-user administration, lifecycle platform operations, SecHub policy/PDP/PEP/PIP views, telemetry mutations, reconcile routes) now consult the PDP at platform scope through `checkPlatformPermission` in `src/security/iaaaPdpSeam.ts` instead of the hand-rolled `isPlatformAdmin` predicate, which is removed. Decisions are behavior-identical (live platform-admin binding at platform scope, unrevoked, unexpired); the permission passed is the platform-privilege grant (`role:assign`). Fail-closed: an invalid actor, an unknown permission, or no live binding denies, and a store/PDP error propagates to a 500 with no data. Per-surface permission mapping is CISO follow-up, not decided here.
