### Added

- **Memory consolidation between `mas_memory`, `mas_learnings`, and VectorMemory (#5060, AG-6):** three memories were written and read independently — a fact learned in chat never became a learning, a learning never became a vector chunk. The bridge is deliberately narrow and states the source of truth: `mas_memory` owns session facts; a fact under a durable prefix (`preference:` / `fact:`, `MEMORY_CONSOLIDATION_FACT_PREFIXES`) that has been stable past `MEMORY_CONSOLIDATION_MIN_AGE_HOURS` (default 24) becomes a `memory`-domain learning; a promoted learning is embedded into Vectorize for recall — the vector stays an index, never a source of truth. Idempotent per fact (`mas_memory:<key>` evidence ref), daily-guarded (`MEMORY_CONSOLIDATION_INTERVAL_HOURS`) like the weekly reflection job, triggered from the chat turn's finish hook, and best-effort: a consolidation failure never fails the turn, and a vector miss keeps the learning durable. Every threshold is externalized (`MEMORY_CONSOLIDATION_ENABLED`, `_FACT_PREFIXES`, `_MIN_AGE_HOURS`, `_SCAN_LIMIT`, `_VECTORIZE`, `_INTERVAL_HOURS`, declared on `Env` and documented in `.dev.vars.example`). Also fixes zone-less SQLite `CURRENT_TIMESTAMP` parsing in the guard (parsed as local time it made a just-written row look hours in the future). Pinned by `tests/memory_consolidation_5060.test.ts`. #clo-arsi
