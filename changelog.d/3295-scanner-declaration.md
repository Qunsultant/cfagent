### Changed

- **The options scanner is declared per broker (#3295, product-neutrality #3292):** the scan **engine** has been broker-neutral since #3272 (`DynamicOptionsScreener` on `OptionChainSource`), but the saved-profile store (#28) and the scan-history store (#27) hold E*TRADE rows, so the scanner surface had no neutral backend. Rather than route another broker into E*TRADE's stores, the capability is now declared: `optionsScanner` joins `BrokerOperation` with E*TRADE `true` and Alpaca `false`, kept deliberately distinct from `optionChains` (which stays neutral for both). `ScannerUnavailableError` (`code: provider_unsupported`, message naming the broker) is defined in `src/client/scannerApi.ts` ahead of the clients, so a non-E*TRADE surface refuses before issuing any request instead of reaching `/api/etrade/options/*`. A broker-keyed profile/history store with its migration remains the follow-up recorded on the issue. #ceo-aimp
