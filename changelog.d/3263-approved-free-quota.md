### Added

- Per-service quota for approved-free accounts (#3263, sub-issue of #3256): a per-service cap per period (stock, goal, options, strategy) plus a limited-time window bounds the operator-granted unlimited-free entitlement (#3245). Migrations 0054 (quota tables) + 0055 (PROPOSED seeds: 10 runs per service per month, monthly window, ask-to-upgrade-or-buy-credits at the limit per #3241 decision 4). The gate checks the quota before the unlimited-free bypass, against counts keyed (tenant_id, login, family, period); at the cap the run is refused with the outcome-encoded `FAILURE_APPROVED_FREE_QUOTA_EXHAUSTED` (402) and the workflow completes as `quota_exhausted` with no approval wait, no screener start, and no checkout. A quota store failure degrades safely: the gate logs a structured aspect event and falls through to the normal gate (fail-closed, no run fails on a store blip). Operator-only GET/POST `/payments/agentic/commercial/approved-free-quota` (one thing per POST: a family cap, the window, or the at-limit action; idempotent upserts; audited with code + message + login), an `ApprovedFreeQuotaSection` in the Billing servicing tab (operator editor + per-account lookup; non-operators read their own quota; expired grants render the catalog-coded expired message), a quota block in the first-run step-5 summary, and quota_exhausted / quota-covered surfacing in the CheckoutPanel poll via the catalog message. AOP: 13 quota outcome codes in the existing outcome catalog (category `commercial`) resolved only through the single resolver — every route failure is a catalog-coded body, the failure kind is recorded structurally at the point of failure as a typed `ApprovedFreeQuotaError`, and the panel renders the catalog businessMessage; no raw error text reaches a client. No cap set for a family means no approved-free runs for that family (fail-closed); the default caps currently-unlimited grants at 10/service/month unless the operator sets otherwise.
