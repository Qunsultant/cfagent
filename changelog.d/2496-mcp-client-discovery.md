### Changed
- Chat turns now consume the platform catalog through the Agents SDK's provided MCP client path (#2496, programme #2494 G1): the OrchestratorAgent registers the `openaimp` server on Think's MCPClientManager and Think merges the runtime-discovered tools into the turn — the hand-wired in-process adapter merge is gone from the turn. Discovered tools carry the SDK's `tool_openaimp_<name>` keys (the prompt steering surface names them), the camelCase catalog aliases are retired, and per-call identity is stamped by the loopback carrier from the turn's resolvers — a `tools/call` with no resolvable acting login fails closed with `mesh_mcp_identity_required` and is never dispatched. CHAT_NLQ_MODE keeps its exact semantics; its mechanism is now a turn gate over Think's `activeTools`. The /mcp handler stamps the verified acting login + traceparent into queued job payloads server-side (mesh-door commands now execute for identified external callers as themselves; anonymous callers still fail closed at the #1744 gate).
