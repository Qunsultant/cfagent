### Added

- **Scope discipline in issue templates (Qunsultant/cfagent#3121):** `.github/templates/bug_report.md`, `enterprise_architecture.md`, and `task.md` now state that every item excluded from an issue gets its own sub-issue under that issue, created when identified and linked under Related issues — never left as a follow-up note, TODO, or 'future work' line; an excluded item with no sub-issue is a review finding. The Completion report follow-ups ledger is tightened so every follow-up lists its sub-issue number (explicit owner deferral with date and reason remains the only escape).
