### Fixed

- AccountPanels broker errors (Broker Alerts, Account Transactions, Watchlists, Broker Orders, Positions) now surface coded business messages from the single outcome catalog instead of raw E*TRADE upstream bodies (e.g. `oauth_problem=parameter_absent` no longer reaches the UI; the raw diagnostic stays in server logs only). New catalog codes: FAILURE_ETRADE_ALERTS_UNAVAILABLE, FAILURE_ETRADE_TRANSACTIONS_UNAVAILABLE, FAILURE_ETRADE_WATCHLISTS_UNAVAILABLE, FAILURE_ETRADE_ORDERS_UNAVAILABLE, FAILURE_ETRADE_ACCOUNTS_UNAVAILABLE, and FAILURE_ETRADE_UPSTREAM. Failure responses changed shape from `{error: <raw>}` (502) to `{code, message, retryable}` with the catalog status: 401 for missing/expired auth tokens, 502 for section upstream failures. Genuine-empty (200) and genuine-not-found (404) payloads are unchanged, and the underlying E*TRADE connection behaviour is unchanged — a missing PROD session still yields no data until the user connects E*TRADE.
