### Added

- **Comparison legs dispatch through the comparison workflow host (#4896):** both production comparison legs — the NLQ leg and the panel comparison route — now run through `startComparisonWorkflow` when the gate holds: the leg's (broker, mode) pair derives from the E*TRADE platform with the same environment resolution the platform was constructed with, the acting login is present, and the residency bindings are configured. The resolving `comparison.run` step writes the comparison result to stage storage, so a host-path run's result is readable after settlement; the step's output contract stays `{ runId }`. Gated-out runs stay on the unchanged sync dispatch, retained as the fallback until the cutover is proven by a live run. Host-path runs write no ledger rows (no D1 comparison-ledger adapter exists — the accepted-absent contract, named and pinned); host-path runs record over the residency's durable stores, so per-stage control from the copilot turn awaits #4859, and publish stage-boundary messages through the residency's queue where the sync dispatch publishes nothing (the workflow norm for durable runs).
