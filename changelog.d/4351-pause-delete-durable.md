### Fixed

- **A paused schedule stays paused, and a deleted one stays deleted (#4348 S6b, #4351):** the schedules the owner paused (`_runDeclaredScheduledTask`, e.g. `options-strategy-scan-observe`) came back active after a restart, and a deleted one reappeared. Both are code-declared Think tasks, re-evaluated on every boot; the user's decision was keyed by the SDK's alarm id, which the declaration never sees. Now the store keys a declared task by its **task id**, the declaration skips a task whose record is `paused` or `deleted` (Think cancels the schedule of a task it no longer sees, so the decision survives every restart), and deleting writes a **tombstone** instead of forgetting the id. Resume flips the record to active and re-runs the declaration (`internal_reconcileScheduledTasks`), so the task is armed again at once. An unreadable store declares nothing (fails closed). #autoclaw-001
