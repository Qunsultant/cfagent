### Changed
- Alpaca options discovery is live (#3388 slice 2). `createDiscoveryService` now takes the trading platform as a **required** argument and derives its chain source from it with `platformChainSource`, so a run always executes on the selected broker's chains — a platform with no chain source refuses at composition, naming itself, instead of borrowing E*TRADE's. `optionsDiscovery` is `true` for both brokers; the read routes and the panel keep their named refusal, which is now inert for E*TRADE and Alpaca and still guards a future broker. Runs remain scoped to the broker that produced them (slice 1).
