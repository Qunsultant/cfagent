### Added

- Commercial product bundles (#2781, Monetization Phase 2.15): named offers of two or more listed PayPerUse products at one operator-set, owner-approved bundle price, sold through the same Selling UX as plans (2.1 Billing mint → confirm-by-retrieve fulfill → status). New bundles land PROPOSED; only an APPROVED bundle price may be charged (402 otherwise). A verified payment confers one entitlement per member product in the same atomic batch — no partial entitlement, ever. Bundle purchases surface in the Billing tab; cancel covers bundle purchases; a bundle payment ref is refused by the refund flow with the coded 422 (refund decision undecided, owner decision pending). Gate consumption of bundle entitlements is a follow-up, not this slice.
- Bundle outcomes are outcome-catalog codes (AOP, #2781): 14 new commercial entries resolved through the single resolver (successes + failures incl. the 402 unapproved-price / not-paid refusals and the 409 purchase-exists replay refusal); bundle prices are decimal USD rounded to cents; migration 0053 adds agent_product_bundles, agent_bundle_purchases, and agent_bundle_entitlements (no bundles seeded).
