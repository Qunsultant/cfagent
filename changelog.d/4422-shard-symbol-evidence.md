### Added

- **Per-symbol evidence for a shard (#4422):** Workflow Runs now shows what each options feed shard recorded about its symbol — the verdict, the skip reason, the target and its expected-move distance, the fit-gate bucket and reasons, one row per strategy type with its evaluated/passed/rejected counts and rejection reasons, the best candidate (expiry, debit, max loss/profit, POP), and the **quoted legs** (side, quantity, strike, expiry, entry price, bid/ask, IV, open interest, volume). The view states plainly that the full fetched chain is not retained: the shard keeps its derived per-symbol evidence and the assembled report is the full-data surface. A shard that recorded no matrix row claims nothing; a skipped symbol still renders its reason, which is the honest answer to "why was no data retrieved?". #autoclaw-001
