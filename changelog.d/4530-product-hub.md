### Added

- Product Hub page (#4530): a dedicated hub at `?tab=product-hub` with four URL-synced tabs (`view=products | selling-options | admin-products | free-runs`, default products), following the StockHub/OptionsHub pattern (Tabs/TabPanel primitives, panels mount on first visit and stay preserved). The hub is the only place for product-domain URLs: the standalone products page retired (its addresses redirect to the hub tabs), and the Platform Admin products and free-runs sections moved into it. Billing, Checkout, Payments, Orders, Ledger, and Revenue stay standalone, exactly as they are. The panels are reused, not rebuilt: the product catalog, the selling section, the product/version administration, and the free-run allowance section. The Marketing draft preview is its own sub-tab of the Products page (the catalog table's Preview draft button composes the draft and opens that tab); the Teaser rules section stays as-is on the Catalog tab.
- **Nested tab levels get their own URL param (#4530):** the catalog's sub-tabs used to sync on `view`, which is the HUB's tab inside `?tab=product-hub` — so clicking "Selling options" in the catalog also switched the hub tab. Inside the hub the catalog syncs on `productTab` (a `tabParam` prop on the panel; standalone callers keep `view`), the retired `?tab=products&view=marketing-draft-preview` address and the nav deep link now carry `view=products&productTab=marketing-draft-preview`, and `applyRoute` resolves a retired address BEFORE adopting a tab so the unknown-id fallback panel never paints for a pass.
