### Security

- **System-principal binding story defined and wired (#5459):** `authorizeSystemPrincipal` in `src/security/serviceDispatch.ts` is the single consultation for non-dispatch consumers (first the ledger gate): a configured service login resolves to the `service:<login>` principal and is evaluated by the existing `authorize()` at tenant scope, with no policy change, no new grant rule, and no new store. The binding that carries a permission is a tenant-scope binding whose role grants it under the existing table (for `ledger:record`, tenant-admin), provisioned through the existing general grant path; a member binding denies, an unconfigured login does not resolve, and a platform-admin binding for the service id is surfaced as an anomaly for audit. G-AUD-3 threading is advanced for the service-actor class (the PDP decision carries the threaded login); no gate is wired by this slice. The binding shape and anomaly semantics are CISO review questions on the PR (#5349 had not decided at build time; no-idle-wait per #5349).
