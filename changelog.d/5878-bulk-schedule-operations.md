### Added

- Bulk pause, resume and run-now for schedules, in bulk and per category (#5878): the Schedules home toolbar gains Pause all / Resume all / Run all now over the whole list, plus the same three verbs over the kind the filter currently shows (Stocks, Options, Strategy, Goal, Payment, Trading, System, Queued, Other). Each action confirms its scope and count first, then runs one `POST /api/schedules/pause|resume|trigger` call with `{ scope }` and shows the per-row summary — counts plus each refused row with its reason. The verbs reuse the single-row management path per row, so derived records (token renewal, retention, nightly research, sweeps, watchdog) refuse with their named errors, goal rows refuse via the goals surface, and the live-feed row pauses/resumes/triggers through its own host; a refusal never stops the rest of the batch. Paused rows stay listed and resumable, as the single-row path already does.
